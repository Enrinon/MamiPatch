<h1 align="center">Issue Guidelines</h1>

**Before opening an issue, please follow these guidelines:**

**1. Be respectful:** Treat everyone politely.

**2. Describe the problem:** Explain what happened and what you expected to happen.

**3. Provide details:** Include your device model, Android version, patch version, and screenshots when reporting bugs.

**4. Avoid duplicates:** Check existing issues before creating a new one.

**5. Stay on topic:** Issues should be related to MamiPatch or its compatibility.

**6. No personal information:** Do not share passwords, private data, or sensitive information.

Thank you for helping improve MamiPatch!
